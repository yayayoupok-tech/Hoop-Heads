# Before and after: the R pass (R1–R10)

Each image shows the build before the R pass (L19, commit 457b134) on the left and the final R10 build on the right, the same screen or moment reached the same way. The numbers are each milestone's own measurement, from CHANGES.md. Where a screen is new, the left side shows what was there before.

## R1 — Retro look: one pixel-art world

- One pixel grid: 1280×720 draws a 640×360 world at k 2, every sprite goes through pixelize() (at most 24 colors, a 1-px outline) and every letter is the Retro Ball Font. The Retro check: 0 smoothed draws, 0 of 96 glyph draws off a whole scale, 0 outline gaps in 400 sprites, 23 colors at most in one sprite. — [r1-title-desktop.jpg](r1-title-desktop.jpg)
- The same on a phone (844×390 at 2×: k 2). — [r1-title-phone.jpg](r1-title-phone.jpg)
- The menus: pixel characters, the Retro Ball Font, the logo with a pixel basketball for its O. — [r1-menu.jpg](r1-menu.jpg)
- A quick game: before, pixel players stood in a smooth arena; now the players, fans, hoops, banners and floor share one grid. — [r1-quick-match.jpg](r1-quick-match.jpg)

## R2 — Size and framing: the 15 m compact court

- A 2 m player: 1/9 of the court → 1/6.82 (the 15 m court, 1v1 only), about 14% → 23.3% of the screen height, heads about 32 → 45 world px. — [r2-hs-match.jpg](r2-hs-match.jpg)
- The pro arena mid-play. The §2 gate after the retune: Pro mirror 1.18 PPP, Legend beats Pro 83%, brute 1.00, sniper 1.72 PPP. — [r2-pro-jumpshot.jpg](r2-pro-jumpshot.jpg)
- On a phone, with the thumb controls under the floor line. — [r2-phone-match.jpg](r2-phone-match.jpg)

## R3 — Career foundation: traits, growth, teams

- Two traits a career out of 17, common to legendary, each with an upside and a cost, on the genes card. Trait balance (200 careers a trait): all 17 in range on the final build. — [r3-genes.jpg](r3-genes.jpg)
- Creating a player: still no height or rating sliders; growth is no longer locked to a position. — [r3-create.jpg](r3-create.jpg)

## R4 — The depth chart

- Before, you played every game. Now only the top rung of your team's depth chart plays the week's game, and 1v1 practice challenges (to 7) move you up; big programs start you lower. — [r4-depth-chart.jpg](r4-depth-chart.jpg)

## R5 — High school

- Tryouts first: a 60-second shootout and a 1v1 against a senior decide varsity or JV (55% of freshmen make varsity). — [r5-tryouts.jpg](r5-tryouts.jpg)
- A six-team district (a double round robin, 10 games), the district playoffs and a 16-team state tournament. — [r5-standings.jpg](r5-standings.jpg)
- The hub in year two: your traits, the week, fatigue, the rival and the depth chart's call. — [r5-hub-year2.jpg](r5-hub-year2.jpg)

## R6 — College

- Offers by program tier; three official visits (the facilities, the coach, where you would start). — [r6-offers.jpg](r6-offers.jpg)
- Marquee games, an eight-program conference and its tournament, the 64-program national tournament (27% of simulated seasons get there), draft stock and NIL deals. — [r6-college-hub.jpg](r6-college-hub.jpg)
- Draft night, against your mock-draft spot. — [r6-draft.jpg](r6-draft.jpg)

## R7 — The pros and your money

- The pro hub: contracts you negotiate, free agency, trade requests, the depth chart. — [r7-pro-hub.jpg](r7-pro-hub.jpg)
- What money buys: a trainer, a coach, nutrition, a home gym, a shoe line. In the simulator a spender earns $39M more for $60M of staff; median net worth $217M against $232M for buying nothing. — [r7-business.jpg](r7-business.jpg)

## R8 — Hype and the press room

- Four answers, none best, each saying what it does. The press test (every "always X" policy within ±8% of the average): at 1,000 careers titles +1.3 / +2.5 / −2.2 / −1.5%. — [r8-press.jpg](r8-press.jpg)
- Hype 0–100 with real costs; THE DAILY DRIBBLE's BUZZ panel lists what your hype is doing for and to you. — [r8-buzz.jpg](r8-buzz.jpg)
